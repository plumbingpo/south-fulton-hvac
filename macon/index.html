How Macon Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix.
